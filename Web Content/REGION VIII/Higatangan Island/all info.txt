Higatangan Island-Higatangan Island is a part of Naval Municipality that is separated by a narrow channel located in the north west tip of Leyte.
The name Higatangan is derived from the word “atangan” meaning to wait for.
Old folks say that the place was where the pirates ambush approaching ships.
