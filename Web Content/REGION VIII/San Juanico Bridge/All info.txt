San Juanico Bridge-a total length of 2.16 kilometers which is second longest bridge spanning a body of seawater in the Philippines after Cebu-Cordova Bridge.
Upon its completion, economists and public works engineers quickly tagged it as a white elephant which was "a possession that is useless and expensive
to maintain or difficult to dispose of", because its average daily traffic was too low to justify the cost of its construction.
