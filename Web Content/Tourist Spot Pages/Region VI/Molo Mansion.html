Molo mansion

Region VI

The Molo Mansion, also known as the Yusay-Consing Mansion, is one of the most outstanding historical homes in the Philippines. Among its most notable features are its high ceilings, Neoclassical balustrades, and decorative carvings. It stands across the town plaza and St. Anne Parish Church in the Molo district. The mansion was constructed in the 1920s and was first occupied by Dona Petra Lacson, who married Estanislao Yusay, Manila's judge of the Court of First Instance at the time.
