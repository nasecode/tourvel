Bega Falls

Region XIII

Bega Falls is a 4 tiered waterfalls system, each tier has different characteristics and features situated in the heart of Bega River system starting from the water head source at Sitio Ugnop near the Puting Bato Peak in the mountains of Barangay Mabuhay.
