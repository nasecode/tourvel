Our Lady Of Piat Basilica

Region II

The Basilica Minore of Our Lady of Piat is one of the eighteen Minor Basilicas in the Philippines. It is located at Poblacion, Piat in the province of Cagayan. The shrine is dubbed as the Pilgrimage Center of Northeast Luzon, and a home to the centuries-old brown Madonna, Our Lady of Piat.

Hotel Carmelita

Place to stay

Featuring a bar, shared lounge and views of pool, Hotel Carmelita is located in Tuguegarao City, 1.3 km from Caggay. Among the facilities of this property are a restaurant, a 24-hour front desk and room service, along with free WiFi throughout the property. The hotel features family rooms. Ruyu is 1.7 km from Hotel Carmelita, while Tuguegarao is 2 km away. The nearest airport is Tuguegarao, 2 km from the accommodation.

Address : Diversion Rd., 3500 Tuguegarao City, Philippines

La Luna grill

Place to eat
