Villa de Babat

Place to stay

Near Naidi Hills When you stay at Villa De Babat in Basco, you'll be within a 5-minute drive of Naidi Hills. This golf guesthouse is 1.3 mi (2.1 km) from Dipnaysupuan Japanese Tunnel and 2 mi (3.2 km) from Vayang Rolling Hills.

Address: 305 Reyes Street Kayvaluganan, Batanes, Basco, 3900

Harbour Cafe

Place to eat

Harbour Café is one of the popular restaurants in the town proper of Basco. It is hardly visible from the main road since there's no particular signage or indication outside, but people are all familiar with the place and they can tell you how to get there. This café is quite small, with just a few tables, but it has a simple and yet lovely ambiance, with an outdoor open-air dining area that offers you a very nice view of the sunset and of the seaport below.

Address: National Rd, Basco, Batanes
