Katibawasan Falls

Region X

Katibawasan Falls in Camiguin is the tallest waterfall on the island. According to the locals, Katibawasan Falls measures about 250 feet high. The waterfall pours down towards a green plunge pool which is quite refreshing where tourists can swim and enjoy the splash of water from the waterfalls.

Camiguin Volcano Houses

Place to stay

amiguin Volcano Houses is approximately 10kms from the airport and city center, and 15mins away by motorbike uphill to Mt. Hibok with breathtaking views on the way up. The property does not serve food nor has a restaurant, but you are free to cook and prepare your food and enjoy a picnic in the garden. The property is situated uphill and away from the hustle and bustle of the city as such, expect a life living with nature.

Address: 6M6X+9XR Camiguin Volcano Houses, Mambajao, Camiguin
