Dahilayan Forest Park Resort

Place to eat

Forest Park’s Picnic Ground is a 1 Hectare area of sprawling pine trees. The areais situated on a sloping hillside, which gives the landscape a cascade of different levels and platforms. The grounds itself is naturally carpeted with thousands of fallen pine needles. Amidst this pine forest, one will find a vast variety of life-like life-size animal replicas from around the world. Picnic tables are sprawled around the entire grounds for guests who prefer to bring in their own food and feast while communing with nature.
