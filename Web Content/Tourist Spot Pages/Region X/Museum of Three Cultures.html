Museum of Three Cultures

Region X

The Capitol University Museum of Three Cultures exhibit and preserve the cultural, historical and significant artifacts of the three dominant cultures of Northern Mindanao, namely the lowland migrants, the Indigenous peoples, and the Muslim Maranao. The museum envisions educating the public to understand and appreciate the diversity of culture and arts.
