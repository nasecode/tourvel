Basilica of our Lady of Peñafrancia

Region V

The Peñafrancia Minor Basilica or the Minor Basilica of Our Lady of Peñafrancia (Spanish: Basílica Menor de Peñafrancia) is a Roman Catholic minor basilica located on the outskirts of Naga City — also known as the Pilgrim City and Queen City of Bicol — in the Bicol Region of the Philippines. It is one of the largest Marian pilgrimage sites in Asia. The icon of Our Lady of Peñafrancia is enshrined in this sanctuary. Rev. Fr. Rodel Cajot is the present Rector of the Basilica, having been appointed in 2011. It is the only basilica in the Bicol Region and the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Caceres.
