Tourist Spot in the Philippines


Intramuros

NCR

Old-world Intramuros is home to Spanish-era landmarks like Fort Santiago, with a large stone gate and a shrine to national hero José Rizal. The ornate Manila Cathedral houses bronze carvings and stained glass windows, while the San Agustin Church museum has religious artwork and statues.

White Knight Hotel

Place to stay

White Knight Hotel Intramuros has a total of 29 rooms. The hotel offers you attractive and comfortable rooms which include 4 types mainly the Deluxe, Standard, Suite and Executive Rooms. All rooms have private bathrooms, personal wardrobe, desk, telephone and free WIFI internet access. In addition, rooms come with all the modern equipments to bring the convenience and safety for guests, such as flat screen TV sets, air conditioners, menu tour, refrigerators and the other necessaries.

Address: White Knight Hotel Intramuros, General Luna Street, corner Urdaneta St, Intramuros, Manila, 1002 Metro Manila

Barbara's Heritage Restaurant

Place to eat

A well-known Filipino heritage restaurant located inside the Walled City of Intramuros. The restaurants boasts the classic architecture popular in the 18th century when the Spanish colonized the Philippines. When it first opened its doors, the restaurant only offered a lunch buffet to accommodate the people who worked at the nearby offices. However, the restaurant quickly gained popularity and they eventually decided to expand their menu to serve more customers. Today, they cook up delicious Filipino and Spanish recipes as well as other European-inspired dishes.

Address: Plaza San Luis Complex, Heneral Luna St, Intramuros, Manila, 1002 Metro Manila
