Venice Grand Canal Mall

NCR

Venice Grand Canal Mall was opened to the public in the year 2015 and it is owned by Megaworld Corporation. The mall is situated at the fifty hectares of McKinley Hill township of Megaworld Corporation in the city of Taguig. The Grand Canal is famous for many reasons: the stunning palaces that line its banks; the luxurious bridges that traverse its murky waters; the way it connects the city's top attractions and, of course, the kitschy good fun gondola rides that allow you to see it all.
