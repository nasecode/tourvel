Tourist Spot in the Philippines


Manila Baywalk Dolomite Beach

NCR

The Baywalk is a popular seaside promenade and beachfront overlooking Manila Bay along Roxas Boulevard in the Manila, Philippines. The Baywalk is a two-kilometre stretch from the US Embassy near Rizal Park up to the Cultural Center of the Philippines just past the Manila Yacht Club.

Golden Bay Hotel

Place to stay

Golden Bay Hotel is a perfect choice for those who has an inadequate budget looking for a hotel that has good facilities and close to all the landmarks Manila has to offer. Golden Bay Hotel is centrally positioned in the heart of the tourists and business districts in a superb complex alongside Roxas Boulevard fronting the U.S. Embassy. Golden Bay Hotel is located 20 minutes from the international airport.

Address: HXGH+GV9 Golden Bay Hotel, Ermita, Manila, 1000 Metro Manila
