Tourist Spot in the Philippines


Rizal Park

NCR

Rizal Park, widely known as Luneta Park is an iconic park that carries with it a rich history, as it is the location where the country's National Hero, Dr. Jose Rizal was executed. The park was also formerly known as Bagumbayan during the Spanish Occupation in the Philippines.

Rizal Park Hotel

Place to stay

Rizal Park Hotel is conveniently located at the hearth of Manila's business, entertainment, convention and shopping districts. It is close to many tourist and historical spots such as Intarmuros, Fort Santiago, Manila Cathedral and others. The hotel is just across the Rizal Park (Luneta National Park), and adjacent to the Quirino Grand Stand, and the Embassy of the United States of America.

Address: Rizal Park Hotel Manila, South Road, Ermita, Manila, 1000 Metro Manila

Cafe Ilang Ilang

Place to eat

The famed Cafe Ilang-Ilang is one of the premier buffet restaurants in the country. The flagship restaurant of the historic The Manila Hotel, it serves local and international dishes, and has private rooms that can be booked for small to mid-size groups.

Address: Cafe Ilang-Ilang, Manila Hotel, 1 Rizal Park, Ermita, Manila, 0913 Metro Manila
