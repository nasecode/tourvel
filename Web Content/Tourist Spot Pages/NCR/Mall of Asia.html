Tourist Spot in the Philippines


SM Mall of Asia

NCR

SM Mall of Asia has a 2-storey entertainment complex with world-class movie theatres and contains the country's first Olympic-sized ice-skating rink. The extensive selection of shops and dining establishments, from fast food to gourmet, can be a bit overwhelming. There's also a small amusement park outside, which is a good place for a picnic and just to chill and enjoy the sea breeze It’s a must to mention that sunsets here are stunning and, on weekends, you get to see a short fireworks display. SM Mall of Asia also includes a convention centre, an expansive concert ground, and an indoor arena with a seating capacity of up to 20,000 people.

Microtel Mall of Asia

Place to stay

Microtel Mall of Asia is located in the Mall of Asia shopping complex in Manila. It features an outdoor swimming pool and restaurant. Free WiFi is provided in the hotel lobby. Rooms and suites at the Microtel Mall of Asia Manila have air conditioning.

Address: Microtel by Wyndham Mall of Asia, Coralway Avenue corner Seaside Boulevard Mall of Asia Complex, Pasay, 1308 Metro Manila

Rico's Lechon

Place to eat

Rico's Lechon serves its best-tasting Cebu lechon together with other popular Filipino dishes ood food and one of the best lechon places in the MOA. The place is clean and the staff are nice. They have a wide variety of dishes to choose from but the lechon is a must try.

Address: GXMJ+F2C Rico's Lechon - SM Mall Of Asia, Marina Way, Pasay, Metro Manila
