Tourist Spot in the Philippines


Star City

NCR

Star City is a 35,000 m² amusement park in Pasay, Philippines. It is located in the reclaimed area of the Cultural Center of the Philippines Complex, part of Bay City. The facility is owned by Star Parks Corporation, a subsidiary of Elizalde Holdings Corporation. Star City is the Philippines only weather proof amusement park.enjoy the 30 exciting rides and spectacular attractions the whole year round! were 70% wheather proof and air conditioned.

Selah Lofts Manila

Place to stay

Known as the "center for commerce and tourism". Lofts Pasay is perfect for business & leisure travelers. This hotel features a space saving architecture in its rooms, windows in some rooms allowing the guests to see the busy city, and a hotel bar and restaurant where the guests can enjoy their breakfasts. Spa massage in the hotel, spa is offered by the hotel. There is no shortage of things to do or food to try within the vicinity of Selah Lofts Pasay. Within short distance from the property are attractions and restaurants travelers can enjoy.

Address: Selah Lofts Pasay, 2004 - 224, David, Pasay, Metro Manila
