Blue Lagoon

Region I

Blue Lagoon is considered to be one of the most enjoyable and romantic spots in the country. It is surrounded by a plethora of fantastic volcanic landscapes, and the water itself is opaque and vividly blue. Rising pillars of steam only add to the spa’s fantastic ambiance

Camiguin Blue Lagoon Cottages

Place to stay

Camiguin Blue Lagoon Cottages stands among the lush rainforest surrounding Taguines Lagoon. It’s literally a place with a calming view of emerald green water right in front of your door/window! And it’s just a 5-min drive from Benoni Port .

Address ; Taguines Lagoon, Mahinog, 9101 Camiguin

Lava Resturant

Place to eat
