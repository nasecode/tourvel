Bangui Windmills

Region I

Even when you search Ilocos in any search engines or in image search, Bangui Windmills will always be present. The magnificent big turbines that supplies electicity became a famous landmark of Ilocos. Bangui Wind Farm is a wind farm in Bangui, Ilocos Norte, Philippines. The wind farm uses 20 units of 70-meter high Vestas V82 1.65 MW wind turbines, arranged in a single row stretching along a 9-kilometer shoreline off Bangui Bay, facing the South China Sea.
