Kawasan Falls

Region VII

It has three waterfall levels, all of which feature natural pools where visitors can swim and relax. Kawasan is not only picturesque, but it is also an excellent place to swim in. Visitors often praise the water as cold and refreshing. The water from the top of Kawasan comes from the Kandayvic Spring. The falls was originally known as “Mantayupan Falls”, named after the barangay it was located in. In the early 1980s, the municipality of Badian decided to develop the area into a tourist spot.
