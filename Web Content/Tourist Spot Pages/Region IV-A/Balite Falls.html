Balite Falls

Region IV-A

For those seeking a refreshing spot to unwind, visit Balite Falls in Amadeo, Cavite. Its enchanting waterfalls are among the several emerging attractions in Cavite. Enclosed inside a 4-hectare property, the Balite Falls has refreshing cold water gushing down a natural swimming pool.

Balinsasayaw Restaurant

Place to eat

Balinsasayaw Restaurant was founded in year 2001 by a local Puerto Princesa City based family. The restaurant was named as such because the owner Rose Pe, is a native of Palawan, it was named after the bird specie living in abundance in Palawan - The Balinsasayaw or cave swifts.

Address: 55 Luksuhin, Silang, Luzon 4118 Philippines
