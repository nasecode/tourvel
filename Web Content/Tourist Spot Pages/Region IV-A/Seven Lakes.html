Seven Lakes

Region IV-A

A visit to San Pablo, Laguna isn't complete without exploring at least one or two of the famous Seven Lakes: Sampaloc, Bunot, Mohicap, Palakpakin, Calibato, Pandin, and Yambo Lake. These popular tourist spots in Laguna are ideal for those who want to relax and are looking for a short escape near Manila.
