Little Santa Cruz Island

Region IX

Little Santa Cruz Island is shortly across the big one . It is a highly protected sanctuary for sea turtles & other rare marine life. There are no hotels or resorts on the island but there are available tables and cottages with grill stations. Overnight camping is not allowed and bringing of liquor and alcoholic drinks are also not allowed.
