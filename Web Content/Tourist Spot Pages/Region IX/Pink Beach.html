Pink Beach of Zamboanga City

Region IX

The Great Santa Cruz Island is also known as the Pink Beach. It offers an amazing pink sand that results from pulverized red organ pipe coral washed up from offshore mixes with white sand. The blue and green shades of the sea and the lush greenery add more beauty to the island. You can indulge swimming, sun bathing, snorkeling, and other outdoor activities.It was recognized by National Geographic as one of the 21 Best Beaches in the World in 2017.
