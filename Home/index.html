Ditumabo Mother Falls

Region lll

Majestic to behold, Ditumabo Falls gushes forth from up high. The dazzling white of its waters contrasts stunningly against the lush green of the rainforest that lies to either side of it.

Kawasan Falls

Region VII

The falls was originally known as “Mantayupan Falls”, named after the barangay it was located in. In the early 1980s, the municipality of Badian decided to develop the area into a tourist spot.

Little Sta Cruz Island Sandbar

Region IX

Little Santa Cruz Island is shortly across the big one . It is a highly protected sanctuary for sea turtles & other rare marine life.

Pink Beach

Region IX

The blue and green shades of the sea and the lush greenery add more beauty to the island. You can indulge swimming, sun bathing, snorkeling, and other outdoor activities.

Higatangan Island

Region VIII

The name Higatangan is derived from the word “atangan” meaning to wait for. Old folks say that the place was where the pirates ambush approaching ships.
